College Launches New Innovation Center

Students get a new space to turn creative ideas into real projects

Our college has announced the opening of a new Innovation Center designed to encourage students to develop creative ideas and practical projects.

The Innovation Center will provide students with modern facilities and resources to work on technology, research, and entrepreneurship projects.

Students from different departments can use the center to collaborate and share their ideas. Teamwork and creativity will be the main focus of the new facility.

According to college officials, the center will organize workshops, competitions, and training programs throughout the academic year.

The center is expected to officially begin its activities on September 1, 2026. Students are encouraged to register for upcoming programs and make use of the facilities.

Source: UNESCO
