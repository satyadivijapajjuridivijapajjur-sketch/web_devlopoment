Jaipur - The Pink City of India

Welcome to Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan and India's first planned city. Known for its stunning pink buildings, royal palaces, and vibrant culture, Jaipur is a perfect blend of history and modernity. This guide will help you explore the best of Jaipur in one trip.

Top Attractions

Jaipur is famous for its architectural marvels that showcase Rajput and Mughal styles.
